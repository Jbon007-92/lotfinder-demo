LotFinder

Lot Saver for Chrome: save lots while you browse Copart, IAAI, eBay, Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist, then compare them all in one place.

← Back to deals

How it works

  1. Browse Copart, IAAI, eBay Motors, Facebook Marketplace or Craigslist the way you always do.
  2. On a lot you like, click the orange LotFinder button in Chrome's toolbar. It reads the page you are looking at (year, make, model, mileage, bid, title, damage, photos) into a short form you can correct.
  3. Tick the damaged parts (it suggests some from the listing's damage description) and save.
  4. Click Compare saved lots: every lot from every site, ranked by estimated margin after fees, repairs and transport. Update a bid as the auction moves, remove lots, or export everything to a spreadsheet.

Install (about a minute)

  1. Download the extension (.zip)
  2. Unzip it. You get a folder called lotfinder-lot-saver.
  3. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the lotfinder-lot-saver folder.
  5. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to LotFinder Lot Saver.

Your data

Saved lots stay in your own Chrome on your own computer. Nothing is sent to LotFinder or anyone else. The extension only reads a page when you click its button, and only on Copart, IAAI, eBay, Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist pages. Photos are kept as links to the auction site, not copied. It never bids, buys or signs in for you.

Margins are estimates from LotFinder's illustrative fee, parts and value tables. The parts list is yours; hidden damage (frame, flood, electrical) does not show in photos. Inspect before you bid.
